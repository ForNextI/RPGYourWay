# RPG Your Way 1.11.6

Start-language, short-window multiplayer, and palette QA.

- Lets **Table Chat** and **Dice** scroll independently when the Play viewport is shorter than their content, including non-fullscreen multiplayer use.
- Gives the Step 1 D&D 5.5e/default system selector the standard rotating **+ / ×** accordion affordance.
- Changes Step 1 wording to **Choose the game system** and **Use this game system**.
- Changes the ready-to-play character popup to **Choose these ready-to-play characters** and explains that the default characters are built for the D&D 5.5e system.
- Changes Paste Information guidance to **Paste your character's information, then add characters one at a time**.
- Renames adjustable gameplay setting **Backstory influence** to **Backstory reveal**.
- Keeps dropdown-number controls forest/olive with lime text on hover and focus rather than flashing white.
- Keeps the Step 4 **Onward** button visible before names are complete, but disabled until both campaign and Game Master names are entered and the rest of setup is ready.
- Replaces the mint-leaning pale green with a warmer olive family: `#d6d1a3`, light `#ebe6c5`, deep `#beb77c`.
- Advances the release from **1.11.5** to **1.11.6**.

# RPG Your Way 1.8.8

Start now begins as a single active board instead of showing later onboarding sections at the same time.

- Step 1 is the only visible onboarding plaque at first.
- D&D 5.5e is preselected and shown as the default.
- Clicking the selected rules button reveals the five available rulesets.
- The old Start-page “Setting: The Uncharted Realms” line is removed from the visible UI.
- “Use these game rules” commits the current selection and replaces Step 1 with Step 2, Gather Your Party.
- Step 3 and Step 4 remain hidden until their preceding steps receive explicit completion actions in later QA passes.
- Step 2 includes the quiet “Or reset everything and start again” escape hatch.

This release intentionally focuses on the Step 1 sequential workflow. Step 2 will receive its own larger workflow pass next.

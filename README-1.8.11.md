# RPG Your Way 1.8.11

Global visual-language pass.

- Replaces the former mint surfaces with a pale olive family derived from the site's olive controls.
- Standardizes raised controls around a thicker brass rim, an inner contrast line, and a separate lower brass/shadow plane.
- Forest and olive faces use a pale inner line; pale faces use a forest inner line.
- Extends the dimensional treatment to small utility buttons, modal controls, account/script/play controls, onboarding controls, and other pressable UI.
- Standardizes form-entry/inset surfaces around the olive -> brass -> parchment treatment where they did not already have a dedicated bezel.
- Strengthens popup/dialog furniture and common fieldset/card surfaces so they read as plaques rather than flat web panels.
- Keeps the header and footer intentionally flat, except for the Ko-fi “Buy Brett a Coffee” control, which is now a full dimensional brass-rimmed button.

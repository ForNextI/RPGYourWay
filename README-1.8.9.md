# RPG Your Way 1.8.9

Step 2, Gather Your Party, now follows the one-board-at-a-time onboarding flow.

- The separate drag-and-drop behavior is removed. Browse opens the normal file picker; pasted character information remains a separate route.
- The names-and-portraits guidance is plain explanatory text directly under the Gather Your Party introduction.
- Party capacity is derived from the party array and displayed live, so adding and removing characters always updates the remaining places.
- Removing or changing characters clears stale capacity messages.
- Re-adding the exact same source file during the same setup is blocked as a duplicate.
- A ready party is confirmed with “Use this party,” which clears Step 2 and reveals Step 3.
- Party-leader selection no longer appears while the party is still being assembled. It now appears at the end of Step 3, after default or customized gameplay settings are complete.
- The quiet reset-and-start-again link remains available at the bottom of the active workflow.

This release keeps character importing and clarification one character at a time.
